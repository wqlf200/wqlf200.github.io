## Visual Design Guidelines

### Color Scheme
- **Primary Background**: Deep black (#000000 or #0a0a0a)
- **Main Accent Color**: Fluorescent/neon green (#00ff00 or #39ff14)
- **Secondary Accent**: Bright gray-green blend
- **Text Colors**: 
  - Headings: Neon green
  - Body text: Light gray (#cccccc) or off-white (#e0e0e0)
  - Links: Neon green with underline on hover

### Typography
- **Font Style**: Modern sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Space Grotesk, or Roboto)
- **Hierarchy**:
  - H1: Large, bold, neon green
  - H2: Medium, semi-bold, neon green or white
  - Body: Regular weight, light gray
- **Styling**: Clean, elegant with neon green emphasis

### Interactive Effects
**Page Transitions**: 
- Fade in/fade out effects between page changes
- Smooth, subtle animations

**Scroll Effects**: 
- None (static scroll)

**Loading Effect**: 
- Retro TV/monitor loading animation
- Old-school CRT screen effect (scanlines, flicker)
- Or simple neon green progress bar

**Button Hover States**:
- Glow effect with neon green
- Slight scale or border animation

---

## Navigation Behavior
**Interaction Method**: Click to navigate (standard web navigation)  
**Page Position After Click**: Top of new page (smooth scroll to top)

## Technical Notes for Stitch
1. **No Images**: Do not include actual photos, avatars, or illustrations. Use placeholder shapes (circles, rectangles) or text labels instead.
2. **Wireframe Focus**: Emphasize layout structure, typography hierarchy, and spacing.
3. **Annotations**: Label all key sections, buttons, and interactive elements clearly.
4. **Responsive Consideration**: Design should be clean enough to adapt to mobile (mention if needed).
5. **Dark Mode Native**: This is not a light mode variant—design entirely for dark theme from the start.
